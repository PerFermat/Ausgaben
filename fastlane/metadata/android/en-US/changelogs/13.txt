Securities: buys, sells and dividends can now be recorded and are written back into KMyMoney - with quantity, price, fees and taxes that may be split across several categories. On top of that the app reads statements straight from the PDF: it recognises the security, the date and the amounts, and while doing so it learns per bank and per portfolio where the values sit in the document. With the first statement it asks for the right label; later every rule can be replaced, extended or skipped through the pencil icon, and a page of its own lists all learned rules for touching up. Several statements can be read in one batch, duplicates are marked in yellow, and the document ends up attached to the booking. Statement recognition is off by default and is switched on in the settings. Also new: several profiles with separate data, their own accent colour and backups by scope; Spanish as the third language; a calculator keypad with haptics and its own layout in landscape; profit and loss against the cost basis in the portfolio plus separate filters for open and closed positions; a free-text search field in the booking filter and the deletion of individual GPS coordinates. Fixed: wrong signs on security bookings and unreadable text in dark mode.
