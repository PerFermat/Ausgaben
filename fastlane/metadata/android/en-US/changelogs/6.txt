SMB/Samba is now set up through a wizard: the app scans the local network for servers, you log in, pick one of the shares it lists and save - no more typing host and share names by hand. The server's workgroup is shown and, in a Windows domain, pre-filled in the user field. Errors name the actual cause. Scheduled transactions: entering or skipping the next date makes it disappear from the list and moves the KMyMoney schedule on by one period on the next .kmy export, instead of deleting the rule. CSV mode: browse buttons for the export and import folder, also during onboarding.
