SMB/Samba: the port can now be chosen freely - as its own field in the wizard, discovered servers bring their port along, and the address accepts smb://host:7777/share. A server that silently accepts a wrong login as guest is now detected and reported instead of failing later when the file is opened; the file browser names the actual cause. Other people's .kmy files are handled reliably: freshly created files without bookings, accounts in a foreign currency (amounts now in the account's currency), tagged transactions and account names used more than once. Unreadable files and foreign CSV formats say in plain words what is wrong instead of quietly doing nothing.
